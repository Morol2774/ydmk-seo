How Our Web Design Process Works

From your first call to launch day – 5 simple steps. You always know where your project stands.

  1. 1

    Free Discovery Call & Website Audit (30 min)

    We connect by phone or meet in person in the GTA. We review your current website (if you have one) and your competitors, then give you a plan and an exact price – no obligation.

  2. 2

    Fixed-Price Contract

    We sign a contract covering price, timeline, payment schedule and service level agreement. You pay a 20–30% deposit and we get started.

  3. 3

    Design & Development

    We show you a prototype and design first – you approve it before any development begins. You get a short status update every week.

  4. 4

    Testing & Launch

    We test speed, forms, analytics and how the site looks on every device. Then we launch on your domain and hand over your logins.

  5. 5

    Payment Plan & Growth

    Your website is already live and collecting leads while you pay off the balance. After that – ongoing support, marketing and growth, based on your plan.

Privacy Policy

How Your Digital Marketing Keys collects, uses and protects your personal information.

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Your Digital Marketing Keys ("we", "us", "our") is a web design and digital marketing studio based in Mississauga, Ontario, Canada. We respect your privacy and handle personal information in line with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email, phone number, business name, website, location, the services you're interested in, budget, preferred call time and anything you write in the request form or send us by email, phone, WhatsApp or Telegram.
  • Project information: content, images, logins and business details you share with us to complete your project.
  • Website usage data: pages visited, device and browser type, approximate location and how you found our site, collected through cookies and analytics tools such as Google Analytics.

2. How we use your information

  • To reply to your request and schedule your free consultation
  • To prepare quotes, contracts and invoices, and to deliver the services you order
  • To provide support, send project updates and manage payment plans
  • To improve our website and services
  • To send news and offers, only if you've agreed to receive them

3. Consent

By submitting our request form or contacting us, you agree that we may use your information to respond to you and discuss your project. You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing us. Withdrawing consent may limit our ability to provide some services.

4. Sharing your information

We never sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with trusted service providers who help us run our business, such as website hosting, email, CRM, payment processing and analytics tools, and only as much as they need to do their job. We may also disclose information when required by law.

5. Storage and retention

Your information may be stored on secure servers in Canada or other countries used by our service providers. We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, for example for tax and accounting records.

6. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, including secure passwords, limited access and encrypted connections. Logins you share for your project are used only for that project, and we recommend changing them once the project is complete.

7. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to understand how visitors use the site and to measure our advertising. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work.

8. Email and messages

We send marketing emails only with your consent. Every marketing email includes a way to unsubscribe, and we process unsubscribe requests within 10 business days.

9. Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where the law allows. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with the date it was last updated.

Terms of Service

The terms that apply when you use our website or work with Your Digital Marketing Keys.

Last updated: September 30, 2026

These Terms of Service apply to your use of this website and to all services provided by Your Digital Marketing Keys ("we", "us", "our"). Each project also has a written contract. If the contract and these terms differ, the contract applies.

1. Our services

We provide website design and development, ecommerce stores, CRM setup and automation, social media management, branding, photo and video content, SEO and advertising management. The exact scope, deliverables and timeline of each project are set out in your quote and contract.

2. Quotes and fixed pricing

After the discovery call, we send a written quote. Once the contract is signed, the project price is fixed and does not change unless you ask for work outside the agreed scope. Any additional work is quoted separately and starts only after your written approval. Prices are in Canadian dollars and do not include HST unless stated.

3. Payments and payment plans

  • Work starts after the deposit (20–30% of the project price, as stated in your contract) is paid.
  • On a payment plan, the balance is paid in equal monthly payments over 6–10 months at 0% interest.
  • Paying the full project price upfront gives a 10% discount.
  • If a payment is more than 14 days late, we may pause work or support until the account is up to date.
  • Monthly plans are billed at the start of each month.

4. Timelines and our deadline guarantee

Your contract includes the launch date. If we miss it for reasons within our control, you receive a 5% discount on the project price for every full week of delay. Delays caused by late content, feedback or approvals from your side extend the timeline by the same amount of time.

5. Your responsibilities

You agree to provide the content, images, logins and feedback we need on time, and confirm that you have the right to use any materials you give us. You're responsible for the accuracy of your business information on the website.

6. Revisions and approvals

You approve the prototype and design before development begins. The number of revision rounds is listed in your contract. Changes requested after approval may be quoted as additional work.

7. Ownership

Your domain, hosting and accounts are registered in your name. Full ownership of the website, design files and code transfers to you on launch day, or after the final payment if you're on a payment plan. Your website stays live the whole time. We may show the finished project in our portfolio unless you ask us not to.

8. Third-party services and costs

Some tools are provided by other companies, such as domain registrars, hosting, Shopify, payment processors, CRM platforms and advertising networks. Their fees and terms are separate from ours. Advertising budgets are paid by you directly and are not included in our management fees.

9. Monthly plans and cancellation

Care, social media and marketing plans renew monthly. You can cancel any plan with 30 days' written notice. The 15% website discount for a 6-month social media plan requires the plan to stay active for the full 6 months.

10. Results and liability

We work hard to grow your business online, but we can't guarantee specific rankings, sales or follower numbers, because they depend on factors outside our control. Our total liability for any claim is limited to the amount you paid us for the service in question.

11. Ending a project

Either side may end a project with written notice. You pay for work completed up to that date, and we hand over everything that has been paid for.

12. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada that apply there.

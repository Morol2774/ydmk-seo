How Our Web Design Process Works

From your first call to launch day – 5 simple steps. You always know where your project stands.

  1. 1

    Free Discovery Call & Website Audit (30 min)

    We connect by phone or meet in person in the GTA. We review your current website (if you have one) and your competitors, then give you a plan and an exact price – no obligation.

  2. 2

    Fixed-Price Contract

    We sign a contract covering price, timeline, payment schedule and service level agreement. You pay a 20–30% deposit and we get started.

  3. 3

    Design & Development

    We show you a prototype and design first – you approve it before any development begins. You get a short status update every week.

  4. 4

    Testing & Launch

    We test speed, forms, analytics and how the site looks on every device. Then we launch on your domain and hand over your logins.

  5. 5

    Payment Plan & Growth

    Your website is already live and collecting leads while you pay off the balance. After that – ongoing support, marketing and growth, based on your plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a website cost in Toronto?

A landing page starts at $2,800, a company website at $6,900, and an ecommerce store at $11,500. Your price is fixed in the contract before work begins and doesn't change during the project.

How does the payment plan work?

Pay 20–30% upfront, then the balance in equal monthly payments over 6–10 months at 0% interest. Your website launches and starts collecting leads after just 2–3 payments. Pay in full and save 10%.

What happens if you miss the deadline?

For every week of delay, you automatically receive a 5% discount on the project price. It's written into the contract.

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain and hosting are registered in your name, and all logins are handed over on launch day (on a payment plan, after the final payment – but your website stays live the whole time).

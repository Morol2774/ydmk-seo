Frequently Asked Questions

Everything about pricing, payment plans, timelines and working with our team. Can't find your answer? Ask us during a free consultation.

Pricing & payments

How much does a website cost in Toronto?

A landing page starts at $2,800, a company website at $6,900, and an ecommerce store at $11,500. Your price is fixed in the contract before work begins and doesn't change during the project.

How does the payment plan work?

Pay 20–30% upfront, then the balance in equal monthly payments over 6–10 months at 0% interest. Your website launches and starts collecting leads after just 2–3 payments. Pay in full and save 10%.

Can I pay for a website or services in monthly installments?

Yes! We offer flexible payment options, including monthly installments for website development and selected services. We want to make professional digital solutions accessible to businesses of different sizes.

Can I choose individual services or a complete package?

Both! You can choose individual services or combine website development, marketing, content creation, advertising, and automation into a package that fits your needs and budget.

Timelines & ownership

What happens if you miss the deadline?

For every week of delay, you automatically receive a 5% discount on the project price. It's written into the contract.

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain and hosting are registered in your name, and all logins are handed over on launch day (on a payment plan, after the final payment – but your website stays live the whole time).

Working with us

How do I get started with Your Digital Marketing Keys?

Simply contact us to schedule a consultation. We'll discuss your goals, recommend suitable services, and outline the next steps, timeline, and pricing before starting your project.

What types of businesses do you work with?

We work with local businesses, startups, and growing companies across different industries. Whether you need a website, social media management, branding, or a complete digital marketing system, we tailor our services to your business.

Can I meet your team in person?

Absolutely! We offer in-person meetings with our developers, social media managers, and designers in Toronto and the GTA. We can discuss your ideas, business goals, and project details face-to-face.

Do you build websites in English, French and Ukrainian?

Yes. We build and support websites in all three languages, which is especially valuable for multicultural businesses across Canada.

My business isn't in the GTA. Is that a problem?

Not at all. We work online with businesses across Canada and worldwide. GTA businesses also get a free in-person meeting and an on-location photo shoot.
